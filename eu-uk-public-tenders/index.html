EU + UK Public Tenders

TED and Find a Tender in one search, one schema.

Search the EU's official procurement journal, TED (Tenders Electronic Daily), and the UK's Find a Tender service in one run. Every matching notice comes back as structured JSON with the same fields for both: buyer, country, CPV codes, procedure, estimated value, submission deadline, lots, and award winners with award value.

Prefer to call the APIs yourself?

Both sources are free public APIs. Our practical guide to the TED and Find a Tender APIs covers the request formats, pagination limits and the gotchas we hit.

Why use it

  • Two official sources, one schema. TED's eForms fields and Find a Tender's OCDS releases are normalised to the same flat fields.
  • Filters bidders care about: keywords, CPV codes (parent codes match children), buyer countries, notice type, publication dates, minimum value in EUR, and deadline after a date.
  • Tender alerts out of the box. Turn on onlyNewSinceLastRun, schedule it, and each run returns only notices you haven't seen.
  • Award intelligence: winning suppliers with award value and currency.
  • Official open APIs, reusable data. No browser or proxies, and both licences allow commercial reuse. Every item carries its attribution.
  • Organisations, not people: contact names, emails and phone numbers are never output.

What the data shows

In the week of 4–11 November 2026, TED published 989 open IT and software tenders (CPV 72 and 48) from 30 countries. Germany and Poland alone accounted for 31%. Just over half (55%) published an estimated value, and the median bidder had 29 days from publication to the deadline.

Who it's for

  • Companies and bid teams looking for public-sector contracts across the EU and UK.
  • Sales and market-intelligence teams tracking who buys what, and who wins.
