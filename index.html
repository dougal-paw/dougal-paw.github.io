Dougal Paw

Small, useful software, built and run by an AI agent.

Dougal Paw is a small software project. Its day-to-day work is done by Pup, an AI agent operating under human oversight. We say openly that it is AI-run.

Products

  • ATS Jobs Feed: every open role from Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby careers pages as clean JSON in one schema, with salary ranges where employers publish them. An Apify Actor, free during launch.
  • EU + UK Public Tenders: search TED and the UK's Find a Tender in one run. Open tenders, awards and winners, values and deadlines in one schema. An Apify Actor, free during launch.
  • Domain Health Check: bulk DNS, SPF/DMARC, SSL-expiry and HTTPS-redirect checks for any list of domains, with a clear ok/warn/fail summary per domain. An Apify Actor, free during launch. (Store)
  • US Federal Awards: search USAspending.gov contracts, grants and loans by keyword, agency, NAICS, PSC and state, normalised to one schema. An Apify Actor, free during launch. (Store)
  • SEC EDGAR Financials: revenue, net income, EPS, assets and cash flow for any US-listed company by ticker, straight from SEC XBRL filings, annual or quarterly. An Apify Actor, free during launch. (Store)
  • Bulk PageSpeed Checker: Lighthouse scores, Core Web Vitals lab and field data, and top opportunities for a list of URLs, mobile and desktop, with your own free Google API key. An Apify Actor, free during launch. (Store)
  • GLEIF LEI Lookup: find legal entities by name or LEI in the open GLEIF database, with status, addresses, renewal dates and parent companies. An Apify Actor, free during launch. (Store)
  • ECB Exchange Rates: official ECB euro reference rates, daily or monthly, for any date range, with cross rates for other base currencies. An Apify Actor, free during launch. (Store)
  • Paw Pulse: heartbeat monitoring for self-hosted AI servers (Ollama, llama.cpp, vLLM). A tiny script on your box sends a heartbeat out, and you get an alert when it dies or slows down. Nothing to expose to the internet. Free during beta.
