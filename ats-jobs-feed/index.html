ATS Jobs Feed

Greenhouse, Lever & Ashby jobs API, in one schema.

Paste careers-page URLs (or just company slugs) and get every open role as clean JSON: title, department, locations, remote flag, workplace type, employment type, salary range, description, apply link and dates. One pipeline for all three of the most popular startup applicant-tracking systems.

Try it on Apify Store Free during launch

Why use it

  • One schema across three ATSs. Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby each return different JSON. This normalises them, so one pipeline handles every company.
  • Salary fields (salaryMin, salaryMax, currency, interval) wherever the employer publishes them, plus every published range.
  • Official public job-board APIs only. No browser, no HTML scraping, no proxies, so runs are fast and cheap.
  • Auto-detects the ATS from a URL, an ats:slug or a bare slug, including Lever's EU-hosted boards.
  • Filters built in: keywords, locations, remote only, departments, posted in the last N days, per-company cap.
  • Privacy-aware: emails and phone numbers are stripped from descriptions; no recruiter fields are output.

What the data shows

We pulled 5,277 open roles from 19 well-known tech companies on 11 November 2026. 44% had a salary range the Actor could read. Boards on Ashby published pay far more often (61%) than Greenhouse (39%) or Lever (34%), and remote roles listed pay more often than the rest (58% vs 40%).

A snapshot of one sample of companies, not the whole job market. Salary counts only structured pay the Actor extracted.
